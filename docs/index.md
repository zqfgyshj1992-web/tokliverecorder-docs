# Tok Live Recorder Documentation

*Last updated: October 15, 2026. Maintained by the Tok Live Recorder team.*

**Tok Live Recorder is a web-based TikTok live recorder.** You paste the link of a public TikTok LIVE that is on air, its servers record the broadcast, and you download the recording as an MP4 in the original stream quality. The recording keeps going after you close the browser tab or lock your phone.

These docs cover how recording works, what the free trial includes, and the limits you should know before relying on it. The product itself runs at the [Tok Live Recorder web app](https://tokliverecorder.com/).

## What it does

- Records a public TikTok LIVE from its link, in the cloud, while the LIVE is on air.
- Saves the broadcast stream itself as an MP4, so your device's on-screen comments, gift banners and notifications are not in the file, and no watermark is added.
- Works in any modern browser on a phone or computer, with nothing to install.
- Also records public Kick LIVE streams on the web. Separate desktop apps for Windows and Mac cover Twitch, YouTube and X.

## What it is not

- **Not a TikTok video downloader.** Downloaders save videos that are already posted. Tok Live Recorder records a LIVE while it is happening.
- **Not a way to recover an ended LIVE.** Once a broadcast ends, there is no public copy left to record. TikTok keeps a replay only for the host, for 30 days ([TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7543897462214269496)).
- **Not a screen recorder.** It does not capture your screen, so it can't include the comments you see while watching.

## Quick start

1. Copy the link of a TikTok LIVE that is on air right now.
2. Open the web app and sign in with Google or email.
3. Paste the link and start the recording.
4. When the LIVE ends or you stop it, download the MP4 from **My Recordings**.

Full steps: [Record a TikTok LIVE](recording.md). Limits: [Limits and file storage](limits.md).

## Who uses it

- **Creators** archiving their own LIVEs before TikTok's 30-day host replay expires.
- **Clip editors** who need an original-quality source to cut highlights.
- **Teams and agencies** keeping a record of channels they manage.
- **Viewers in other time zones** who can't watch a LIVE as it happens.

Record only your own broadcasts or streams you have permission to save, and follow TikTok's terms.
