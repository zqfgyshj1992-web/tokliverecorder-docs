# Record a TikTok LIVE

This page explains how to record a TikTok LIVE with Tok Live Recorder, step by step, and what happens to the stream while it records.

## Before you start

- The LIVE must be **public** and **on air**. Recording starts from the moment you press start; earlier parts of the LIVE can't be captured.
- You need to be signed in (Google or email). The free trial allows 3 recordings a day, up to 30 minutes each, one at a time.

## Steps

1. **Copy the LIVE link.** In the TikTok app, open the LIVE, tap **Share**, then **Copy link**. A LIVE link usually looks like `tiktok.com/@creator/live`.
2. **Paste the link.** Open Tok Live Recorder in any browser and paste the link into the box on the homepage.
3. **Start recording.** Check your remaining allowance, then start. The recording now runs on the server.
4. **Leave it running.** You can close the tab or lock your phone. Recording continues until the LIVE ends, you stop it, your per-recording limit is reached, or the stream becomes unavailable.
5. **Download the MP4.** After processing finishes, open **My Recordings** and download the file. On a phone, the file may be in Files or Downloads rather than Photos.
6. **Check the file.** Open it and confirm the sound and length before the cloud copy is deleted.

## What happens during a recording

When you paste a link, the service resolves the public broadcast address that TikTok already serves to viewers and copies that stream to a file as it arrives. It does not film a screen and does not re-encode, so the MP4 has the resolution and bitrate the platform was broadcasting. A short preview clip on the site is optimized for playback; the full download is the source-quality file.

Because the recording copies a live stream:

- It can only start while the broadcast is on air.
- It stops when the broadcast ends, because the stream address stops serving video.
- Graphics that are part of the broadcast itself remain in the video; your own device's interface does not.

## Recording Kick LIVE

Kick LIVE works the same way: paste a public Kick LIVE link and start recording. Kick also keeps replays (VODs) on the streamer's channel for 7 days, or 30 days for verified channels ([Kick Help Center](https://help.kick.com/en/articles/7112432-kick-vods-stream-replays)), so check the channel's Videos tab first if you missed a stream.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The link is rejected | The LIVE has ended or is not public | Check that the creator is live right now |
| The recording stopped early | Per-recording limit reached or the stream dropped | Check the length limit; start a new recording if the LIVE continues |
| The beginning is missing | Recording started after the LIVE began | Start as soon as the creator goes live |
| Can't find the file on a phone | Browser saved it to Files or Downloads | Look in the Files app or the browser's downloads list |
