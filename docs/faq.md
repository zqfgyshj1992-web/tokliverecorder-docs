# FAQ

## How do I record a TikTok LIVE?

Copy the link of a LIVE that is on air, paste it into Tok Live Recorder, start the recording, and download the MP4 from My Recordings when it finishes. See [Record a TikTok LIVE](recording.md).

## Do I have to keep the page open while it records?

No. The recording runs on the server, so you can close the tab or lock your phone.

## Can I record a TikTok LIVE that already ended?

No. Once a LIVE ends, the stream stops being served and there is nothing left to record. If you hosted it, TikTok keeps a replay for you for 30 days.

## Is there a free way to try it?

Yes. After signing in with Google or email, the free trial allows 3 recordings a day, up to 30 minutes each.

## Will the comments and gifts be in the video?

Your device's on-screen comments, gift banners and notifications are not in the file, because the service records the broadcast stream rather than your screen. Graphics that are part of the broadcast itself remain.

## What format and quality are the recordings?

MP4 at the quality of the stream TikTok was broadcasting. There is no watermark and no re-encoding.

## How long are recordings kept?

Cloud recordings are deleted after 7 days. Download them within that time.

## Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It runs in the browser, so there is no app or APK to install.

## Can I record someone else's LIVE?

Record only your own broadcasts or streams you have permission to save, and follow TikTok's terms.
